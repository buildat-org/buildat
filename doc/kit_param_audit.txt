The Bone Yard laying its road on a noise ground (flatten off; tried
2026-11-21, not done): Brimstone is a 2D game in kit/controls' plane
horizontal. The car is held at one height (brim_car steps x and z only),
w:under finds the floors (gravel, mud, the finish line that counts laps)
at the car's height, and the walls stop it only where their depth spans
the plane's height. A road on the hills needs the car to follow the
ground (move.lua's plane, the client's prediction), floors and walls met
at the local height, and the straights and bends cut into short tilted
pieces (several times the ~260 children, over the type's 600 cap, the
build and its landing tick growing with them). Not a quick change.
